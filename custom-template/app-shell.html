Robotics & Mentoring Org · Southern California

Building robots. Building engineers.

SoCal Robotics & STEM Foundation is a nonprofit supporting robotics teams and STEM education across Southern California. We provide mentorship, tools, and competition support to students who want to design, build, and program real machines — no prior experience required.

What we do

01

Mentor & teach

We coach rookie FTC and FLL teams, run workshops, and bring robotics into classrooms across Southern California — because someone did the same for us.

02

Design & build

Every arm, drivetrain and mechanism is engineered, machined and iterated by students — from CAD to competition day.

03

Code & strategize

Our programmers write autonomous routines and drive software from scratch, then scout and strategize alliance by alliance.
